The problem

what the solver on this page is optimising

Optimal control problem

\[ \min_{u[\cdot]}\; \ell_f\big(x[N]\big) \;+\; \sum_{n=0}^{N-1} \ell\big(x[n],\, u[n]\big) \] \[ \text{subject to}\quad x[n+1] = f\big(x[n],\, u[n]\big), \qquad x[0] = x_0 \]

State and control

\[ x = \begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \\ \theta \\ v \\ \delta \end{bmatrix} \in \mathbb{R}^5, \qquad u = \begin{bmatrix} u_0 \\ u_1 \end{bmatrix} \in \mathbb{R}^2 \]
  • x, y — position (m)
  • θ — heading (rad)
  • v — speed (m/s)
  • δ — steering angle (rad)
  • u0 — acceleration (m/s²)
  • u1 — steering velocity (rad/s)

Nonlinear dynamics

Continuous time — a kinematic bicycle model, nonlinear in \(\theta\), \(v\) and \(\delta\):

\[ \dot{x} \;=\; f_c(x, u) \;=\; \begin{bmatrix} v\cos\theta \\ v\sin\theta \\ v\tan\delta \\ u_0 \\ u_1 \end{bmatrix} \]

Discretised with a forward Euler step, which is the \(f\) the solver actually uses:

\[ x[n+1] \;=\; f(x, u) \;=\; x + \Delta t \cdot f_c(x, u) \]

Stage cost

\[ \ell(x, u) \;=\; \underbrace{\left(\sqrt{x^2 + y^2 + \varepsilon} - r\right)^{2}}_{\text{distance from the circle}} \;+\; \underbrace{\left(v - v^{\star}\right)^{2}}_{\text{speed error}} \;+\; \underbrace{w\left(u_0^2 + u_1^2\right)}_{\text{control effort}} \]

Final cost

The same, without the control term — there is no \(u[N]\):

\[ \ell_f(x) \;=\; \left(\sqrt{x^2 + y^2 + \varepsilon} - r\right)^{2} \;+\; \left(v - v^{\star}\right)^{2} \]

Parameters in this run

\(\varepsilon\) smooths the square root: the derivative of \(\sqrt{s}\) is undefined at \(s = 0\), which is exactly the origin of the circle cost.
